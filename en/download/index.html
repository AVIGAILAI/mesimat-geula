The game is made for a computer connected to a projector. If you opened this link on a phone, send it to the classroom computer and download it there.

Here's how it looks in class ▶

  1. 1
    Download the game to the classroom computer, the one connected to the projector.Just once. It's best to save the file on the desktop.
  2. 2
    Double-click the file, and it opens in the browser.Chrome or Edge recommended. For full screen, press F11.
  3. 3
    Split the class into teams, choose a level and start.Once the file is on the computer, no internet is needed.

What's in the game

  • Five gates through the story of the Alter Rebbe's arrest and Geula: questions, true or false, word puzzles, riddles and a timeline.
  • A Wheel of Surprises between the gates, with charades and challenges for the whole class.
  • Three levels: Basic (grades 1-3), Medium (grades 4-5) and Advanced (grades 6-8).
  • You can customize everything: questions and answers, your school's name and logo, points, and what appears on the wheel.
  • Clear step-by-step instructions are inside the game.
